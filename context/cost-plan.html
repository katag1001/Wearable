Step 3

Month by month

Each month's trials pay nothing that month. The ones who stay pay from the next month, and keep paying until they cancel. Yearly payers pay the whole year up front. "Needed to break even" is how many paying users that month would cover all of that month's costs (including the new trials' free month), at your prices after the stores' cut: orange when there are fewer, green when there are enough. It spreads each yearly payment over its 12 months, so a month can show a profit (yearly payments arriving up front) while still being under this number. The last column is what that month's trials bring over their whole time with you, after their own costs and the stores' cut.

How it's worked out

What you pay

  • Every month, whatever happens: Vercel $20, Cloudinary $99 (Plus plan), MongoDB Atlas from $30, Expo $19, email $7. That's $175 before anyone signs up.
  • Weather is free: the forecast comes from MET Norway and the typical weather from NASA POWER, both fetched by your server and shared between users in the same area. The only cost is a little server use, already in the Vercel figure.
  • Grows with users: Gemini, about $0.0044 a photo analysed. Cloudinary (photos, storage, viewing) and the database move up to bigger plans as usage grows: Cloudinary $249 at 225+ credits, the database $57, $146, $394, $760 as it passes 5, 10, 20, 40 GB.
  • Month 1 only: Apple Developer Program $99 (covers the year) and Google Play $25 (once, ever). GitHub is free.

Per user

    Measured figures

    • Cloudinary, 30 days to 12 Nov 2026: 130 background removals at 75 transformations each; 3.2 MB stored a piece the old way (2.5 MB of it the original).
    • Gemini: 122 photos cost $0.54, so $0.0044 a photo.
    • Database: 98 clothing pieces had 5,672 saved outfits at about 1.4 KB each. Outfits grow with clothing count to the power 2.5, about 21,000 at 200 pieces.
    • Phone cut-out: tested on Android in November 2026, about a second a photo.

    Assumptions

    • A phone cut-out stores about 0.75 MB a piece; a Cloudinary fallback 3.2 MB and 85 transformations. The 0.75 MB is an estimate until it's measured live.
    • A Cloudinary credit is valued at $0.44. Everyone views photos the way you do (0.17 GB a month).
    • Leavers' data is deleted after the months you set. Prices are US dollars before VAT.
    • Prices come from third-party summaries dated November 2026: check them before launch.

    Sources: Vercel · MongoDB · Cloudinary credits · Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite · MET Norway terms · NASA POWER · Expo · Apple Developer · GitHub · RevenueCat renewal rates
